Emile Djohi is the French editor for QuantEcon's translation of Python Programming for Economics and Finance. An economist, Penn PDRI-DevLab alumnus, and PhD applicant, Emile studies how climate extremes, economic shocks, and structural stressors shape conflict, migration, and welfare, with a focus on low- and middle-income countries in Africa, Central America, and South Asia.
